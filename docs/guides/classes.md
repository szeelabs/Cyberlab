# Classroom mode

For teachers, trainers and companies: run one lab for a whole group and follow everyone's
progress live.

## For the teacher
1. Open **Classes** → **Teach a class**, give it a name, pick a lab, **Create class**.
2. Share the class **code** (for example `K7QM-2XPA`). Codes avoid look-alike characters
   (no 0/O or 1/I/L), so they are easy to read out in a room.
3. Click the class to see the **Students** table, updating every 10 seconds: how many
   tasks each student solved, who completed the lab, their machines, and when they last
   answered.
4. **Reset lab** removes a student's lab machines and files so they can start again with
   fresh answers. **Close to new students** stops new people joining with the code.

You see progress only. Students' files, terminals and answers stay private: a teacher
cannot open a student's workspace.

## For students
1. Open **Classes** → **Join a class**, enter the code, **Join**.
2. Click **Start my lab**. You get your **own** machines and your **own** answers (a
   classmate's flag won't work for you). **Continue** brings you back later.

## Good to know
* Each student's machines count against their own limits, like any lab.
* With real sign-in, the teacher sees each student's email address; in development mode
  everyone is the same local user, so try classes with real sign-in.
* Everything is audited: creating, joining, closing and resetting.
